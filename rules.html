PRIVATE PURCHASE ACCESS LOTTERY — OFFICIAL RULES

Release: October 10, 2026. These rules govern applications for the opportunity to purchase selected collaboration products; this is not a free-product giveaway.

01 / ORGANIZER AND ELIGIBILITY

The lottery is administered by Surplus Research. Entry is open worldwide to individuals aged 18 or older, where participation and the subsequent purchase and delivery are legally permitted. Participation is void where prohibited. Entrants are responsible for complying with applicable local laws. The organizer may decline purchase access where legal, payment, shipping, or trade restrictions prevent fulfillment.

02 / ENTRY PERIOD

Entries close on October 10, 2026 at 09:00 Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+09:00). The server determines whether an entry was received before the deadline. An incomplete or unsuccessful submission is not an entry.

03 / HOW TO ENTER

Submit a valid email address and choose one preferred product on the official lottery website. Jacket applicants must also select a size (S, M, L, or XL). Agree to these rules and the privacy policy, and complete anti-bot verification. Limit one entry per person; duplicate, automated, fraudulent, or otherwise abusive entries may be rejected or disqualified. No purchase or marketing subscription is required to enter. Opting into marketing does not improve the odds of selection.

  • SR-MODIFIED SPEC TNT GORE-TEX® 3L JACKET (GRAPHITE GRAY) — USD $425
  • SR-002 3.1 TNT (SPECTRA LIME) — approximately USD $295

Prices are indicative and may exclude shipping, import duties, taxes, and payment charges. Final prices and availability will be confirmed before purchase. Selection grants an opportunity to purchase, not ownership of a product or a free item.

05 / SELECTION AND AVAILABILITY

After the entry period closes, eligible entries will be randomly selected by preferred product. Jacket selections will be made separately for each requested size, subject to the available quantity for that size. Selection is limited by stock and is not guaranteed. If fewer eligible entries exist than available units, some or all eligible applicants may be selected. Entries do not transfer between products or jacket sizes.

06 / NOTIFICATION AND PURCHASE

Selected applicants will be contacted using the email address submitted. Selected applicants will receive a private purchase invitation by email and have 24 hours from the time the notification email is sent to complete payment. The email will state the exact deadline and checkout instructions. If payment is not completed by that deadline, the allocation may expire and be offered to another eligible applicant. Applicants are responsible for checking their email, including spam folders. Submission or selection does not guarantee successful payment or delivery.

07 / FAIRNESS AND DISQUALIFICATION

The organizer may verify eligibility and invalidate duplicate, automated, fraudulent, or otherwise noncompliant entries. The organizer may adjust, suspend, or cancel the lottery where necessary for security, technical failure, legal compliance, or circumstances beyond reasonable control, subject to applicable law. Any material changes will be communicated on the official lottery website.

08 / PERSONAL INFORMATION

Entry details are used to administer the lottery, check eligibility, select applicants, and communicate purchase access. Marketing emails are sent only where the entrant separately opts in. See the Surplus Research Privacy Policy for further information about personal data handling.

09 / QUESTIONS

For inquiries, contact Surplus Research through the official website. Nothing in these rules excludes rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable consumer or privacy laws.
